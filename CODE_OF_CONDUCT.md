# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

- Be respectful and constructive in all interactions
- Welcome contributions from anyone regardless of background
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people
- Accept that maintainers make final decisions on what gets merged

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information
- Deliberately submitting malicious skills or code

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported to ntudu040@gmail.com.
Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or ban users at their discretion.
